SectionHeading — the recurring "eyebrow + italic serif title" pattern used to open every page section (hero, About intro, Writing index, Contact).

```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="Risk · Governance · AI" title="Making sense of risk in an AI-shaped world." />
<SectionHeading size="sm" title="Recent talks" />
```

The eyebrow is always the accent color and uppercase; the title always uses the italic display serif. `size="sm"` is for headings nested further down a page.
