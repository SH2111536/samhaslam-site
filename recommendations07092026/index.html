Weekly recommendations · 07 Sep 2026

Last leg, then home.

The final stretch of the road trip: wrapping up Skjåk, then on to Valdres for a few nights somewhere with a fireplace and, by all accounts, a genuinely spectacular view. Thursday, a flight from Oslo back to Manchester puts you home for the back half of the week — in time, all being well, for Bolton v Cardiff on Saturday. The West Ham game Tuesday is almost certainly one for your dad to cover alone; you'll still be in a Valdres farmhouse with better views than the away end.

Music

Cardinals — "Masquerade"

A Cork four-piece with a debut full-length that's been quietly picking up serious critical attention for its emotional weight — indie rock that earns its bigger moments rather than reaching for them. Solid company for the last long drive of the trip.

Reading

Massif — Garth Nix

Nix's first adult novel: a ship crew navigating star-system combat astride enormous, star-faring creatures called Massifs. Scale and invention are the whole appeal here, much like his younger-reader work, just with the brakes off. Standalone, no series commitment required before boarding the flight home.

Screen

Collision (HBO Max)

Brand new prestige drama, just landed — early word is exactly the kind of serious, well-built television that tends to reward a proper sit-down. Save it for once you're back and the jet lag's settled rather than trying it on a plane screen.

Comedy

Larry Dean: Hellbent

Touring from 8 September — sharp, self-deprecating Scottish stand-up with a decent reputation for actually being funny rather than just clever. Worth a look once you're back in the country and checking what's on nearby.

Podcast

Latent Space

The very first thing this list ever recommended, back in July — worth returning to now the AI infrastructure conversation has moved on. Good long-haul-flight listening, appropriately enough.

Days out Valdres, Norway

Bitihorn hike

One of Valdresflye's signature peaks, accessible without needing Besseggen-level commitment — a proper mountain, a manageable day, and views straight into Jotunheimen. A fitting last big walk before the trip folds back into ordinary life.

Food & drink Valdres, Norway

Valdres Gjestegard

A restaurant inside the old Valdres prison, doing traditional Norwegian and local Valdres cooking properly — homemade medisterkaker, a well-regarded Wiener schnitzel, and mountain trout in sour cream sauce that keeps coming up in the same breath as "highly regarded." A good last proper meal of the trip.

Food & drink Bolton, matchday

Bolton Market Food Hall

A newer addition to matchday options — Japanese, Caribbean, Indian, Italian and gourmet burgers all under one roof, so nobody has to agree on a cuisine before Cardiff City on Saturday. A change of pace from the usual pre-match carvery routine.
