Weekly recommendations · 12 Oct 2026

Two Londons, one Phil Wang.

A rough one on paper: London and back Tuesday, London and back again Thursday, both the kind of day that starts before six and doesn't end until gone seven. Saturday's a bit of local tech support for someone who needs it, and Sunday you're off to the Opera House for Phil Wang — already sorted, so this list builds around it rather than competing with it.

Music

Teenage Fanclub — "Do Not Dare To Dream"

Decades into it and still writing a proper melody — gentle, unhurried guitar pop that won't ask much of you at six in the morning on a cold platform. Reliable in the way only a band this experienced can be.

Reading

A Wall Is Also a Road — Annalee Newitz

Standalone science fiction from a writer who spends as much time thinking about real technology as imagined futures — close enough to the day job to be interesting, far enough from it to actually switch off. Built for two long train days.

Screen

The Lowdown, Season 2

Billing itself as comedic drama rather than picking one or the other — based loosely on a real Tulsa investigator, sharp enough to hold attention after a thirteen-hour day that started with a train to Crewe.

Podcast

Hidden Brain

Routinely rated the best science podcast going, for good reason — the kind of thing that makes a dull commute feel like it's doing something for you. A proper change of register from the usual AI/football rotation.

Food & drink London, Euston

Yatri, Euston

A different Euston stop from last time — reliably excellent Indian cooking on Chalton Street, a few minutes' walk from the station. Good for either of this week's two trips, should the timing allow.

Food & drink Manchester

Lucky Cat

Gordon Ramsay's pan-Asian spot on King Street, with a proper pre-theatre menu running 5–7pm — a two-minute reason to arrive in Manchester a little early on Sunday, direct hit on your favourite cuisine before Phil Wang does his best to ruin your evening in the good way.

Food & drink Chorley area

The Spinners at Cowling

Consistently well-reviewed, properly local — the sort of place that doesn't need two brutal commute days as an excuse, but will happily serve as the reward for surviving them.

Days out Chorley area

Duxbury Woods

A gentle riverside walk along the Yarrow, woodland the whole way — exactly the sort of low-effort Saturday morning that fits around an afternoon of tech support and doesn't ask anything of legs that did two London round trips already this week.
