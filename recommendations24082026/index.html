Weekly recommendations · 24 Aug 2026

Norway, at last.

The big one. Flight to Oslo Tuesday, then the seven-bases-in-eighteen-days road trip properly begins — first a few nights at Bjonnepodden near Skjåk, then west to a container house with an ocean view near Skiftun, then on to Stormhyttene på Lykling on Bømlo. Morris is at the kennels, the cats are at the cattery, and the only real job this week is driving unhurried between beautiful places. This list splits accordingly: a few things for the car, and a few for the actual ground you'll be standing on.

Music

Buffalo Traffic Jam — "Pictures Of You"

A folk debut built on family memories and unhurried storytelling — sounds like a much older band than they are. Mellow rather than driving, which suits long fjord-side roads better than anything with a mosh pit in mind.

Reading

The Whitechapel Full Moon Society — Elizabeth DeLozier

A maid at a Whitechapel gentlemen's club, three strange house rules, and a body count that turns out to be connected to Jack the Ripper — monsters, curses and family secrets layered under real Victorian history. Standalone, and exactly the right amount of "otherness" for a cabin evening with the fire on.

Screen

Average Joe, Season 2

Darkly comic series about a blue-collar plumber whose late father stole millions from the Russian mafia — now on the run again as a fresh threat closes in. Funnier than the premise sounds, tense enough to actually hold attention on a jet-lagged evening.

Podcast

Dwarkesh Podcast

Long-form, properly technical interviews with the people actually building frontier AI — denser than the usual rotation, which makes it good company for the longer driving legs where you actually want something to chew on.

Food & drink Skjåk, Norway

Spiseriet, Skjåk

A farm restaurant close to Bjonnepodden, cooking with organic produce from Gudbrandsdalen and curing their own wild reindeer, wild sheep and deer. About as far from a tasting menu as it gets, and exactly the kind of local, unshowy food you two seek out on holiday.

Food & drink Stord, Norway

China House, Stord

A reliable stop on the Bergen-to-Bømlo drive, by local account — worth timing the Stord leg around, if only to break up the miles with something other than a service station sandwich.

Days out Bømlo, Norway

Siggjo hiking trail

Bømlo's highest point at 474 metres, visible from most of the island, with a moderate climb that rewards the effort with proper Sunnhordland views. The kind of scenery North Sweden spoiled you for — this should hold its own.

Days out Bømlo, Norway

Espevær

A tiny, car-free island community out beyond Bømlo itself, houses clustered together against the weather — precisely the sort of quirky, characterful, out-of-the-way place you and Holly gravitate to over anywhere polished. Worth the boat trip.
