Weekly recommendations · 31 Aug 2026

Fjords, still.

Leah's birthday Monday, your dad's Tuesday — both marked from a moving car, most likely. The road trip carries on from Bømlo up into Sunnfjord, a few nights under Storehesten itself near Bygstad, before doubling back to Skjåk for round two. Nothing scheduled, as ever, beyond the driving and the views — so this week's list is almost entirely about where you'll actually be standing.

Music

Essie and The Hum

Ethereal indie folk out of Portland, all space and quiet melody — the kind of thing that suits a slow morning with a fjord view rather than anything you'd play at six-a-side. Mellow mode fully justified this week.

Reading

Bodies of Magic — Freya Marske

Out 15 September, so more a pre-order than a pack for the trip, but worth flagging now: a magical medical school where a cohort of healer mages find one of their own dead on day one of the Grand Exam. Inventive magic system plus a murder mystery is a combination you rarely see done properly — this reportedly is.

Screen

Youth (HBO)

Sharon Horgan as a fifty-year-old divorcee juggling her ailing parents, a should-be-grown son, and the small matter of wanting sex and love again — sharp, funny, and honest in the way her best work usually is. Good evening-in-the-cabin viewing once the WiFi cooperates.

Podcast

The Rest Is History

Tom Holland and Dominic Sandbrook, wildly enthusiastic about history in a way that's somehow never dry — a genuine change of pace from the AI rotation, and appropriately long-form for the drive between bases. Vikings settled this exact stretch of coast, as it happens, if you want an excuse to look up an episode.

Days out Bygstad, Norway

Storehesten hike

The mountain you're sleeping under, more or less — an 11km round trip from the centre of Bygstad, about three hours each way, ending at 1209 metres with the Jostedal Glacier, the Åflot Glacier and the North Sea all visible at once. Sturdy boots non-negotiable. This is the Sweden-honeymoon feeling, on demand.

Food & drink Sunnfjord, Norway

Westerås Gard

A farm restaurant in the wider Sunnfjord area — worth a detour if the timings work, for the kind of unfussy, ingredient-led food that farm kitchens tend to do better than anywhere with a laminated menu.

Days out Skjåk, Norway

Storseter Falls

Back in Skjåk territory by the weekend — this waterfall trail is a proper reset after days of driving, with a path that runs right behind the falls themselves. Short enough to fit around unpacking at the new base.

Food & drink Skjåk, Norway

Skeid Kro

A different Skjåk stop to last time — locally reared, self-produced beef, and a help-yourself hot meals counter that leans hearty rather than fussy. Good after a waterfall walk, better after a mountain.
